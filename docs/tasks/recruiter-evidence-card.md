# Recruiter evidence card

Use Marek Nowak’s seeded BrightLabs application to explore what the submitted
materials tell a recruiter about practical experience and individual contribution.

- The example employer can open the evidence card in the existing candidate record.
- The card quotes the offer and the submitted materials, and identifies the
  missing information about Marek’s own decision.
- The recruiter can request, edit, reject or approve a prepared AI question.
  Approval reveals a clearly labelled sample reply and lets the recruiter
  record how useful that reply was. The sample reply covers the original question.
- Existing stage actions and other records keep their current behavior.

## Follow-up iteration

Use three separate gaps: discovery impact, measurement, and individual ownership.
Each suggestion has an independent edit, accept/reject, and send flow.
The candidate responds to each sent question with text or video and to the
measurement task with a file or screen and microphone recording. The employer
can inspect the actual submitted reply and rate it independently.
